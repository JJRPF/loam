## Phase 3: Real OSM topology and 3DEP elevation (plan section 5)

5.1 OSM trail graph:
- `scripts/import_osm_graph.py` (pyosmium 4.3.1) reads `data/osm/new-hampshire-latest.osm.pbf`, the same extract GraphHopper uses. It keeps ways with `highway` in the plan's set (`area=yes` excluded), plus ways tagged `snowmobile=*` and members of `route=snowmobile` relations (1,233). Three passes: relations, node-use counts (numpy), then ways with locations. A way is split at every node used two or more times across kept ways, and at its endpoints (`split_points`). Output goes to `trail_segments` (COPY) with OSM node ids as `from_node`/`to_node`, the way's tags, `category` and the snowmobile/steps flags. `edges` is upserted per way id from OSM (geometry, length, tags, `category`, `osm_source = TRUE`), overwriting curated values. Edges whose way id isn't in the extract keep their row (heat history) but lose their geometry. Every run is recorded in `osm_imports`, which is the cache key for the Phase 4 graph.
- Local run: **342,967 ways kept, 427,226 junction nodes, 720,205 segments, 50 s**. By segment: paved 624,705 (31,606 mi), singletrack 62,091 (6,972 mi), doubletrack 25,105 (5,836 mi), gravel 8,304 (1,573 mi). Locally no edges rows were outside the extract.
- `app/surface.py` `classify_surface(tags) -> SurfaceClass(category, snowmobile, informal, steps)` is now the single classifier. `routing.classify_surface` (GraphHopper details), OTM, "new to me" and the import all use it, and name-based guessing is gone. Rules follow plan 5.1. Choices the plan doesn't cover: `cycleway` is singletrack with a natural surface and paved otherwise; a way with nothing known is paved (conservative for the road cap); `informal=yes` keeps the path's category but sets a flag (the Phase 4 graph uses it). Round 4 regression roads, checked against their tags in the extract, are paved: Grandview Road (unclassified/asphalt, residential/untagged, unclassified/untagged), Ridgewood Drive (residential/untagged, residential/unpaved) and Bettys Lane (residential/untagged, residential/asphalt). `test_api.py::test_surface_classification` asserted residential + gravel = "gravel"; per the plan, any road class is paved, so I updated it.

## Phase 4: Routing (plan section 6)

6.1 Route builder (`app/route_builder.py`): every candidate, from any source, becomes ordered `Piece`s (coordinate range + OSM way id + tags) and goes through `build_route_response`. That computes distance, the surface strip and breakdown (from `app/surface.py`), retraced stretches, shape, road % and access miles, names from OSM `name` tags, 3DEP elevation and `geometry_max_offset_m`. GraphHopper paths are split into pieces from their `osm_way_id` details. Graph loops and seeds give pieces directly. Shape thresholds are Round 6's. Choices the plan leaves open:
- **Access stretch:** the paved stretch at each end (up to the first trail piece) counts as access, capped at **2 mi per end and 40% of the route**. Beyond that it counts as road. Road % excludes access; access is shown on the card as "X mi road to trail".
- Way lengths for naming count only non-retraced steps, and names don't repeat a suffix ("Loop Loop").

6.2 Graph loop search (`app/trail_graph.py`): `TrailGraph` is loaded from `trail_segments` around the start (blocked ways removed by `is_blocked` and a hard-restriction query on `access`/`bicycle`/`foot`). Junctions are OSM node ids. It's contracted to its 2-core and into chains, so dead ends never enter a loop. `search_loops` runs a randomized DFS from up to 10 trail-touching base nodes near the start. **Deviation:** the plan doesn't fix a search order. A single DFS spent its whole budget under one base, so it's round-robin across bases, capped at 2 s and 3M visits, with `max_stem` = 0.2 × target. Loops are deduped at 70% shared length. Graphs are cached per region tile, radius bucket, sport class and `osm_imports` id (LRU 6). `db.latest_osm_import_id` has a 30 s TTL. Deleted `app/contracted_graph.py` (the hand-built graph) and `app/start_suggestions.py`. Nearby-start suggestions now come only from graph-verified trail-touching nodes.

6.3 `passes_hard_gates` gained `geometry`: the max distance from any route vertex (densified) to the union of its claimed OSM ways must be ≤15 m. A route with no claimed ways, or whose ways have no geometry, gets `None`, which fails. The deleted "Bow Forest Grand Loop" coordinates (the screenshot bug) fail it (test). Straight jumps between distant points can't pass, because the densified jump leaves the ways. Failing routes are never shown, not even as a fallback.

6.4 `select_options`: drops candidates >50% off the target or <25% of it, everywhere including the fallback. Dedup is length-weighted (70%). Round 6 composition is kept. `plan_route_options` runs graph loops, GH corridors/sectors and seed loops (Phase 5) in parallel. It widens when the raw pool is under 40 or selection comes up short (a second graph seed, more sectors). When fewer than 5 options survive, the response carries `notice` ("Only N routes match; try a different start or distance.") plus a graph-verified nearby start. ResultsPanel shows it. The SSE `complete` event carries both.
