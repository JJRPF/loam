Sharing routes

You can share a planned route with a link or with friends. Share only routes you have the right to share. Anyone with a link you make can open the route, so only send it to people you trust with it, and stop sharing if you change your mind (see the Privacy policy). A shared route is a computer suggestion like any other, and the same safety warning applies to it: whoever you share it with decides whether to ride it and does so at their own risk. Do not use sharing or friend invites to send spam or to harass anyone.
